# HealthFlow Pitch Deck
## Pharmacy and Hospital Operations Platform

### Slide 1 — Title
**HealthFlow**
Digital operations platform for pharmacies and hospitals

- Pharmacy dispensing, inventory, POS, and NHIS claim support where configured
- Hospital patient records, clinical workflows, and claims
- Multi-tenant, role-based, cloud + offline capable

---

### Slide 2 — The Problem
Healthcare facilities still struggle with fragmented operations:

- Separate systems for pharmacy, claims, inventory, and patient records
- Manual billing and NHIS/insurance claim preparation
- Poor visibility into stock, expiry, sales, and receivables
- Limited offline continuity for branch and remote operations

---

### Slide 3 — Our Solution
**HealthFlow** unifies core operations in one platform.

- Pharmacy workflow: dispensing, sales, inventory, medicine catalog
- Claims workflow: NHIS, insurance, CCC, tariff, and export support where configured
- Hospital workflow: patient records, clinical operations, reports, and claims
- Multi-tenant architecture with role-based access

---

### Slide 4 — Pharmacy Value Proposition
**For pharmacies, HealthFlow digitizes core retail and dispensing workflows.**

- Fast POS and cashier operations
- Stock control with batch, expiry, and supplier tracking
- Replenishment and inventory visibility
- Receipt printing and audit-friendly transactions
- Optional e-pharmacy storefront for OTC and prescription ordering

**Business value**
- Reduced stock losses
- Faster dispensing and checkout
- Better compliance and reporting
- Improved customer experience

---

### Slide 5 — Hospital Value Proposition
**For hospitals and clinics, HealthFlow brings operational control and data continuity.**

- Patient registration and visit history
- Clinical workflow support and record access
- Claims and NHIS processing support
- Inventory and pharmacy integration for hospital pharmacies
- Reporting views across configured departments

**Business value**
- Better coordination between clinical and pharmacy units
- Faster claims processing
- Stronger financial and operational visibility
- Support for growth across facilities and branches
