HealthFlow PharmacyAbout This Manual

Welcome to HealthFlow

HealthFlow brings the main activities of a pharmacy into one secure workspace. This manual explains the everyday tasks your team will perform and the controls that protect your records.

Use the access given to you Each user should sign in with their own account. Available menus and actions depend on the user's role. Do not share administrator accounts or passwords.

Who should use this guide?

Daily users

Pharmacists, pharmacy technicians, medicine counter assistants, cashiers, claims officers, inventory officers, accounts officers, nurses, doctors, records officers, and other approved staff.

Supervisors

Administrators, pharmacy owners, branch managers, procurement officers, warehouse staff, billing officers, and facility managers.

2. Roles and Access

HealthFlow uses role-based access. A user only sees the modules and actions needed for their work. Your facility administrator assigns roles.

Typical roleMain responsibilities
AdministratorFacility settings, staff access, all modules, sensitive corrections, backups, Recycle Bin restore, and permanent deletion.
PharmacistDispensing, clinical review, inventory, patients, claims, NHIS, and reports.
Pharmacy Technician / dispensaryDispensing support, inventory work, patient service, and permitted sales tasks.
CashierPOS checkout, payment recording, receipts, and assigned shift activities.
Inventory / Procurement OfficerStock records, purchase orders, batch receiving, transfers, and expiry monitoring.
Claims / Billing OfficerInsurance and NHIS claim preparation, verification, attachments, submission, and follow-up.
Accounts OfficerAccounting, receivables, reconciliation, payment records, and financial reports.
Branch ManagerBranch oversight, approved inventory and POS actions, reports, and offline sync monitoring.
Clinical / Records StaffPatient records, vitals, medication history, follow-ups, and authorized claim support.
NHIS deletion is administrator-only Rejecting and deleting are different actions. Deleted NHIS claims first move to the administrator Recycle Bin. Restore accidental deletions there; only permanent deletion from Recycle Bin cannot be undone.

Account safety

  • Use your own account for every transaction.
  • Never disclose your password, NHIA credentials, branch token, or verification codes.
  • Lock or sign out of HealthFlow when leaving the computer.
  • Do not use an administrator account for ordinary cashier work.
  • Report unexpected access, unfamiliar transactions, or suspicious prompts immediately.

When a menu is missing

A missing menu usually means your role does not include that permission. Ask the administrator to review your assigned role. Do not use another person's account as a workaround.

HealthFlow PharmacyDashboard and Inventory

3. Dashboard and Inventory

Use the dashboard

Dashboard

The dashboard summarizes current activity. Depending on your role, it may show sales, claims, inventory alerts, recent transactions, low stock, expiring medicines, and quick actions.

  • Review low-stock and expiry warnings at the start of the day.
  • Use date and branch filters where available.
  • Select a summary item to open the related detailed screen.

Add a medicine

Inventory > Add Drug
  1. Search for the medicine first to avoid duplicates.
  2. Select Add Drug and enter the medicine name, category, form, strength, and applicable codes.
  3. Enter batch information, expiry date, quantity, cost price, selling price, and reorder level.
  4. Review the details and save.

Receive or update stock

Stock should normally be increased through a completed purchase or approved stock operation. Keep each batch separate when expiry dates or costs differ.

Batch control

  • Record the correct batch number.
  • Use the printed expiry date.
  • Do not merge different batches.
  • Investigate negative or unusual quantities.

Price control

  • Confirm cost and selling prices.
  • Use approved NHIS prices and codes where applicable.
  • Restrict price changes to authorized staff.
  • Document stock adjustments.

Import inventory from Excel

Inventory > Import Excel
  1. Use the HealthFlow import template or the column format supplied during onboarding.
  2. Upload the workbook and review the preview.
  3. Correct rejected rows, duplicate codes, invalid dates, or missing prices.
  4. Confirm the import only after the preview totals are correct.
Expiry and quantity accuracy Inventory information affects dispensing, purchasing, alerts, claims, and reports. Never guess a batch, expiry date, medicine code, or quantity.

HealthFlow PharmacySales

4. Sales and Shift Management

Open a cashier shift

Sales (POS)
  1. Confirm the selected branch and terminal.
  2. Select Open Shift or Start POS.
  3. Enter the opening cash accurately.
  4. Confirm before beginning sales.

Complete a sale

  1. Search by medicine name, code, batch, or scan the barcode.
  2. Select the correct medicine and batch. Confirm expiry, quantity, and price.
  3. Add items to the cart and enter the quantity dispensed.
  4. For insurance or NHIS transactions, link the correct patient and follow the claim workflow.
  5. Select the payment method: cash, mobile money, card, insurance, or another configured option.
  6. Review the total and select Complete Sale.
  7. Issue or print the receipt where required.
Clinical responsibility remains with the dispenser HealthFlow assists with records and warnings. The pharmacist or authorized professional must still confirm the medicine, dose, interactions, legal requirements, and appropriateness before dispensing.

Correct a cart before payment

Edit the quantity or remove the item before completing the sale. After payment, follow your facility's authorized return, reversal, or correction process. Do not create an undocumented stock adjustment to hide an error.

Close a shift

  1. Finish or suspend all open sales.
  2. Count cash and verify non-cash payment totals.
  3. Select Close Shift.
  4. Enter counted cash and a note for any difference.
  5. Review the shift summary and confirm closure.

When the internet is unavailable

If your branch has offline support, HealthFlow may offer Save to Branch Server, Save Offline Sale, or similar controls. Continue only when the screen confirms the sale was saved locally. Sync when connectivity returns.

Do not repeat a sale after a slow response First check the sales list or receipt status. Repeating the transaction can create duplicate sales and incorrect stock.

HealthFlow PharmacyPatients and Purchases

5. Patients and Purchases

Create or update a patient

Patients > Add Patient
  1. Search by name, phone, folder number, insurance number, NHIS number, or Ghana Card.
  2. If no matching profile exists, select Add Patient.
  3. Enter the full name, phone number, folder number, date of birth, gender, and other available details.
  4. Add insurance or NHIS information exactly as shown on the member's records.
  5. Review and save.
Avoid duplicate patient profiles Always search before creating a patient. Duplicate profiles split prescription, refill, claim, and payment history.

View patient history

Select View History to review medicine lines from sales, general insurance claims, and matching NHIS visits. NHIS medicines show prescribed quantity, served quantity, and serving status. The detailed history loads only when opened so the patient list remains responsive.

Open the patient profile to review authorized details such as purchases, medicines, claims, vitals, refills, and follow-up information. Visibility depends on your role.

Create a purchase order

Purchases > New Purchase Order
  1. Select or add the supplier.
  2. Enter the supplier invoice number and purchase date.
  3. Add each medicine, quantity, batch, expiry date, and cost price.
  4. Compare the item total with the supplier document.
  5. Save the order, then complete or receive it when the physical stock has been verified.

Receiving checklist

  • Supplier and invoice are correct.
  • Medicine and strength match the order.
  • Quantity received is correct.
  • Batch number is readable.
  • Expiry date is acceptable.
  • Cost price is correct.
  • Damaged items are excluded.
  • Purchase total matches the invoice.
Complete purchases only after verification Completing a purchase updates inventory. If the stock has not physically arrived or has not been checked, keep the order pending according to your facility procedure.

6. Insurance Claims

The general Claims module manages configured insurance providers. The separate NHIS module contains the specialized NHIA and CLAIM-it workflow.

Claims module

Use for general insurance claim creation, patient linkage, provider selection, submission status, payment tracking, and follow-up.

NHIS module

Use for NHIS member verification, CCC generation, medicine coding, prescription attachment, CLAIM-it transfer, and CXF export.

Create a general insurance claim

Claims > New Claim
  1. Select the patient and insurance provider.
  2. Confirm the member or policy information.
  3. Link the correct sale or add the approved claim items.
  4. Attach supporting documents where required.
  5. Review amount, service date, and claim details.
  6. Save as draft or submit according to your facility's process.

Understand claim status

StatusMeaningTypical action
DraftInformation is incomplete or still under review.Edit and complete required fields.
ReadyClaim has passed local readiness checks.Submit or export using the approved route.
SubmittedClaim has been sent to the insurer or processing system.Monitor acknowledgement and status.
AcceptedClaim has been accepted for processing.Track payment or reconciliation.
RejectedClaim was declined or intentionally rejected.Review reason and correct where permitted.
PaidPayment has been recorded.Reconcile against remittance.
FailedTechnical submission did not complete.Read the error, correct the issue, and retry safely.
Never delete a claim to hide an error Use correction, rejection, or audit notes. Permanent deletion is reserved for authorized administrators and should only be used for a genuinely invalid duplicate or test record.

HealthFlow PharmacyNHIS

7. NHIS and CCC Verification

Create an NHIS claim

NHIS > New Claim
  1. Search for the patient or enter the NHIS member number or Ghana Card number.
  2. Select the correct card type.
  3. Select Generate/Validate CC Code via NHIA.
  4. Review the returned member name, HIN, gender, date of birth, and membership result.
  5. Complete the folder number, service date, prescribing facility, referral information, prescriber, and authorization details.
  6. Add the medicines and confirm NHIS codes, quantities, approved prices, dose, frequency, and duration.
  7. Attach the scanned prescription before final pharmacy-claim submission. Initial saving or dispensary dispatch may be incomplete.
  8. Review the NHIS Pharmacy Check panel and resolve blockers.
  9. Select Save Details, Send to Dispensary, or Serve Directly.

Choose the correct claim action

ActionResult
Save DetailsSaves a draft without sending it to the dispensary.
Send to DispensaryCreates Pending Serving work for dispensary. Missing medicines or attachment can be completed later on the same claim.
Serve DirectlyMarks all entered quantities served, bypasses the dispensary queue, and moves the claim to Served / Claim Ready. It does not check or change inventory.
Direct serving does not remove final claim requirements The Claims Officer may correct the claim later, but pharmacy submission remains blocked until the prescription attachment and other mandatory details are complete.

CCC generation

CCC generation checks the member against the NHIA eligibility service using the facility's saved NHIA credentials. Each facility must use credentials officially issued for that facility.

HealthFlow screen
>
HealthFlow secure service
>
NHIA eligibility service
CCC generation is not claim submission A successful CCC verifies attendance or eligibility information. The completed claim must still be saved and later transferred or exported using the approved CLAIM-it workflow.

Common CCC results

ResultWhat to do
CCC generatedVerify the member details, complete the claim, and save.
Membership inactiveDo not generate a claim code. Ask the member to contact NHIA or renew membership.
Member not found / invalid numberCheck the card type and number, then try once more.
Facility credentials rejectedAsk the administrator to verify and re-save that facility's NHIA credentials in Settings.
Network or service unavailableKeep the claim information, wait, and retry. Do not repeatedly click the button.
Do not share NHIA credentials Credentials belong to the facility. Enter them only in the protected Settings screen and never send them through chat, screenshots, email, or printed manuals.

8. CLAIM-it Transfer and CXF Export

Direct transfer through the branch server

Direct CLAIM-it transfer is an API operation. No claim file is moved. HealthFlow sends the claim information in the request body, including the CLAIM-it claim ID and supported attachment data.

HealthFlow browser
>
Local branch server
>
CLAIM-it on pharmacy computer
The browser must not contact CLAIM-it directly All direct CLAIM-it submissions must pass through the local HealthFlow branch server. Do not change local ports, service installation, tokens, or bridge addresses unless HealthFlow support instructs you.

Submit selected NHIS claims directly

  1. Confirm that CLAIM-it and the HealthFlow branch server are running on the pharmacy computer.
  2. Open NHIS and select the ready claim or claims.
  3. Choose the configured direct transfer action.
  4. Review the claim count and totals.
  5. Submit once and wait for the CLAIM-it response.
  6. Read the import report and correct any rejected fields before retrying.

CLAIM-it claim ID

Every direct claim requires a CLAIM-it claimID. HealthFlow creates or preserves this identifier when the claim is prepared. If the identifier is missing, HealthFlow blocks submission and asks the user to regenerate or repair the claim.

CXF export is manual import only

A .cxf file is used for the separate manual import workflow. Export the CXF from HealthFlow, then import that file through the CLAIM-it user interface.

MethodWhat is transferredUser action
Direct transferClaim JSON or XML sent through the local branch server.Select claims and submit; no file movement.
Manual CXF importA generated .cxf file containing the claim package.Export from HealthFlow and import in CLAIM-it.
Hosted HealthFlow and local CLAIM-it The online HealthFlow website can generate CCC through the configured service. Direct CLAIM-it transfer still requires the local branch server because CLAIM-it runs on the pharmacy's own computer.

HealthFlow PharmacyAttachments and Corrections

9. Attachments and Claim Corrections

Attach a scanned prescription

NHIS > New Claim or Edit Claim > Scanned Prescription
  1. Scan or photograph the full prescription clearly.
  2. Select the attachment area and choose the file.
  3. Wait until the file name and size appear in the claim.
  4. Save the claim and wait for a success message.
  5. Reopen the claim and confirm the attachment is still listed when the document is important to submission.

Supported files

PDF, JPEG, or PNG, subject to the current screen limit. The NHIS claim screen indicates a maximum of 3 MB per prescription attachment.

Image quality

The patient's details, prescriber, facility, date, medicines, dose, signature, and authorization marks should be readable.

Correct an existing NHIS claim

  1. Open the claim and select the edit action.
  2. Read the warning or validation panel.
  3. Correct only the inaccurate or missing information.
  4. Replace or add the prescription attachment if required.
  5. Select Save Corrections and wait for confirmation.
  6. Recheck readiness before export or submission.
A slow save does not always mean failure Do not click Save repeatedly. Wait for the response, then check the claim list or reopen the claim before trying again. Repeated saves can create confusion or duplicate uploads.

Reject versus delete

ActionUse whenResult
RejectThe claim should remain in history but cannot proceed.Status changes to rejected and the record remains auditable.
DeleteAn administrator confirms the record is an invalid duplicate, test, or mistake.The claim moves to Recycle Bin and can be restored until an administrator permanently deletes it there.
Permanent deletion cannot be undone Verify the archived claim number, patient, service date, and reason before permanently deleting it from Recycle Bin.

Patient Care

11. Reports and Accounting

Generate a report

Reports
  1. Select the report category or dashboard.
  2. Choose the organization, branch, date range, status, and other filters.
  3. Select Generate or apply the filters.
  4. Review summary values and open drill-down details where available.
  5. Export to PDF, Excel, or CSV, or print the report.
Reports reflect selected filters Before sharing a report, confirm the date range, branch, payment type, claim status, and other active filters. Include these details when discussing totals.

Accounting workspace

Accounting

Authorized users can review shift totals, receivables, claims, payments, reconciliation, and accounting summaries.

  • Match shift totals with cashier records and physical cash.
  • Match card and mobile money totals with provider statements.
  • Track insurer and NHIS receivables separately from cash sales.
  • Record payment references and dates accurately.
  • Investigate differences before marking reconciliation complete.

Exporting data safely

Before export

  • Confirm the intended recipient.
  • Use only the necessary date range.
  • Remove unnecessary patient information.
  • Check totals against the screen.

After export

  • Store files in an approved location.
  • Do not leave reports on public computers.
  • Do not send confidential files through unapproved channels.
  • Delete temporary copies securely.

HealthFlow PharmacyAdministration

12. Settings and Administration

Settings are restricted to administrators because changes can affect the entire facility, billing, integrations, and staff access.

Facility profile

Settings > Facility / Organization
  • Maintain the correct facility name, type, contact details, address, and logo.
  • Confirm branch information before assigning users or recording stock.
  • Keep license and accreditation details current.
  • Review the change before selecting Save Settings.

Staff and access

  • Create one account per person.
  • Assign the least privilege needed for the person's job.
  • Remove or suspend access promptly when staff leave or change roles.
  • Review administrator accounts regularly.

Recycle Bin

Recycle Bin
  • Deleted inventory items and NHIS claims are archived instead of disappearing immediately.
  • Administrators can restore accidental deletions.
  • Use permanent deletion only after confirming the record is no longer required.

NHIA and CLAIM-it settings

  • Enter only credentials officially issued to the facility.
  • Keep CCC generation settings separate from CLAIM-it submission settings.
  • Test CCC verification after saving credentials.
  • Use the installed local branch server for direct CLAIM-it transfer.
  • Keep CXF export configured as manual file import where that workflow is used.
Do not change technical installation values casually Branch tokens, local service addresses, CLAIM-it bridge values, ports, encryption keys, and service installation are managed during deployment. Contact HealthFlow support before changing them.

Backups

  1. Use the configured backup controls at the agreed schedule.
  2. Confirm the latest backup date and status.
  3. Download or store recovery files only in an approved secure location.
  4. Contact support before restoring data unless your administrator has an approved recovery procedure.
Backup is not the same as sync Sync sends pending records between approved systems. A backup is a recovery copy. Your facility may need both.

13. Online, Offline, and Sync

Understand the operating modes

ModeMeaningUser action
Online cloudThe browser is connected to HealthFlow's hosted data services.Work normally and wait for success confirmations.
Online local syncThe branch server is available and can coordinate local operations and synchronization.Use approved local actions and monitor sync status.
Offline localInternet access is unavailable, but approved records can be saved locally.Continue supported work and sync when connectivity returns.

Use Offline Sync

Offline Sync
  1. Review connection status, pending records, failed records, and last successful sync.
  2. Select Sync Now only when the connection is stable.
  3. Allow the process to finish without closing the browser or branch server.
  4. Review failures and read the exact error before retrying.
  5. Contact support if the same record fails repeatedly.

Offline PIN and facility LAN security

  • Each staff member uses a separate 6–12 digit offline PIN; the cloud password is never copied locally.
  • Five failed attempts lock offline access for 15 minutes and every attempt is audited.
  • Administrators enable, reset, or revoke offline access in Settings.
  • Multi-computer access requires TLS status Ready and a trusted workstation enrollment. Without TLS, the branch server stays local-only.
Do not reinstall or reconfigure the branch server during an outage A temporary network or remote service problem does not normally require changing ports, tokens, credentials, Windows services, or CLAIM-it. Preserve the working installation and report the exact error.

Avoid duplicate records during slow connections

  • Click Save, Submit, or Sync once.
  • Wait for a success or failure message.
  • Check the list before repeating the action.
  • Search by patient, claim number, sale number, or date.
  • Report duplicates instead of deleting them without authorization.

Local CLAIM-it availability

CLAIM-it runs on the pharmacy computer. Direct transfer works only when the local CLAIM-it service and HealthFlow branch server are available. A hosted website cannot directly operate another computer's local CLAIM-it installation.

14. Troubleshooting

ProblemCheck firstRecommended action
Cannot sign inEmail, password, internet, and assigned organization.Retry carefully, then use password recovery or contact the administrator.
Menu or button is missingUser role and selected branch.Ask the administrator to review access. Do not use another account.

Direct CLAIM-it submit failsBranch server, CLAIM-it, claim readiness, and claimID.Read the error. Repair missing claimID or correct claim data; do not send CXF through the API.

Information to provide support

  • Facility and branch name.
  • User role, not the user's password.
  • Date and time of the problem.
  • Screen or module where it occurred.
  • Claim number, sale number, or record ID when applicable.
  • Exact error message and a screenshot with secrets hidden.
  • Whether the computer was online, offline, or using the branch server.
  • Steps taken immediately before the error.
Never send secrets in a support screenshot Hide passwords, NHIA API keys and secrets, branch tokens, encryption keys, patient documents, and other confidential values unless an approved secure support process specifically requires them.
