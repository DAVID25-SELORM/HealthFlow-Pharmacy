HealthFlow NHISRoles and Access

Assigned Roles and Extra Privileges

HealthFlow separates a staff member's work mode from extra permissions. This keeps NHIS work controlled without blocking authorized staff.

Assigned Roles

Assigned roles are the main work modes a staff member can switch between.

Dispensary Assistant Claims Officer Administrator

If a staff member has more than one role, they choose the active role from the top bar.

Super Admin setup: Tenant Admin > expand facility > Edit User > Assigned roles.

Facility Admin setup: Settings > Staff/User Management > Edit staff.

Additional Privileges

Privileges are extra permissions added on top of a role.

Refunds Inventory Purchases Stock adjustment NHIS deletion

Use privileges carefully, especially NHIS deletion and purchase approval.

Important: If a staff member is in dispensary mode, dispensary restrictions still apply. To perform Claims Officer work, the staff member must be assigned the Claims Officer role and switch to that role.

Example

Sarah can be assigned both Dispensary Assistant and Claims Officer. When Sarah works as dispensary, she serves medicines only. When Sarah switches to Claims Officer, she can prepare and review claims.

Role Safety Rules

  • Do not give NHIS deletion to ordinary staff.
  • Do not give purchase approval unless the person is authorized to approve purchases.
  • Assign the Claims Officer role for full claims-officer work. Do not rely only on the Claims privilege.
  • Facility NHIA credentials are configured once by admin and used by permitted users.

Claims Officer: Save, Send, or Serve Directly

Save, Send, or Serve Directly

1. Open NHIS Claims

Go to NHIS > Claims and click New Claim.

2. Enter Member Details

  • NHIS member number or Ghana Card number
  • Card type and HIN
  • Surname and other names
  • Folder number
  • Gender and date of birth
  • Attendance details where applicable

3. Generate or Enter CC Code

Use Generate/Validate CC Code via NHIA. The facility NHIA credentials are saved in Settings by the administrator.

4. Enter Requested Medicines

Click Add Medicine and enter the prescription details.

  • Medicine code or name
  • Prescribed/requested quantity
  • Unit and unit price
  • Dose, frequency, and duration
Cost visibility: The Claims Officer should see the requested medicine cost before sending the claim to the dispensary.

5. Choose the Next Action

  • Save Details: save a draft without sending it.
  • Send to Dispensary: create Pending Serving work for dispensary.
  • Serve Directly: mark all entered quantities fully served and move directly to Served / Claim Ready.
Direct serving does not check or deduct inventory and does not wait for dispensary. Pharmacy submission still requires the prescription attachment and all mandatory claim details.

1. Open Pending Serving Claims

Claims served directly by a Claims Officer are excluded from dispensary work and cannot be edited in dispensary mode.

Go to NHIS > Claims and open a claim with status Pending Serving.

2. Review Prescribed Medicines

The dispensary assistant sees the medicines entered by the Claims Officer. Dispensary should not freely add new medicines unless the facility policy allows it.

3. Enter Actual Served Quantity

For each medicine, click the serve/edit button and enter the quantity actually given to the patient.

HealthFlow NHISReview and Alerts

Claims Officer Review and Safety Alerts

After Dispensary Serving

The Claims Officer reviews the claim after dispensary saves medicines.

  • Compare prescribed quantity against served quantity
  • Check reasons for partial or not served medicines
  • Confirm claim total
  • Confirm patient and CC details
  • Confirm prescriber details
  • Attach prescription where required
The final claim value is based on served medicines and served quantities, not only the requested medicine list.

Patient Return Alert

HealthFlow may show a Patient Return Alert when the same patient visited within the configured window, such as 24 hours.

This alert appears even if the medicine is different.

  • Review previous visit date and time
  • Review branch, staff, claim status, and medicines
  • Select a reason before continuing if required

Duplicate Medicine Alert

If the same medicine is supplied again within the configured duplicate window, HealthFlow may show a duplicate medicine warning. This is separate from the Patient Return Alert.

Common Override Reasons

Follow-up treatment Doctor changed medicine Patient complaint Emergency Other

When Not to Proceed

Do not finalize a claim if required patient details, prescriber details, served quantity, or attachment information is missing.

HealthFlow NHISPermissions and Troubleshooting

What Each Role Can Do

Dispensary can Serve medicines
  • Open pending serving claims
  • Record served quantity
  • Mark partial, not available, or not served
  • Save medicines for Claims Officer review
Dispensary cannot Finalize NHIS claims
  • Generate or change CC code
  • Change patient NHIS details
  • Freely add new NHIS medicines by default
  • Delete NHIS claims
Claims Officer can Prepare and review claims
  • Register/search NHIS patients
  • Generate or enter CC code
  • Enter requested medicines
  • Save incomplete details without dispatch
  • Send to Dispensary
  • Serve directly without inventory changes or dispensary input
  • Review served medicines
Admin can Control sensitive access
  • Assign roles and privileges
  • Configure NHIA credentials
  • Control NHIS deletion
  • Control purchase approval
