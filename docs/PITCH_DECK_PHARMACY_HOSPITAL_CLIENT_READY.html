HealthFlow

Digital operations platform for pharmacies and hospitals

1. Executive Summary

HealthFlow is a multi-tenant platform designed to modernize pharmacy and hospital operations through integrated workflows for dispensing, inventory, patient records, claims, and reporting.

HealthFlow helps facilities reduce manual inefficiencies, improve compliance, and support growth with role-based cloud and offline capabilities.

2. Pharmacy Solution

  • Fast POS and cashier workflows
  • Inventory management with batch, expiry, and supplier tracking
  • Receipt printing and audit-friendly transaction history
  • Support for OTC and prescription workflows through e-pharmacy

3. Hospital Solution

  • Patient registration and visit history
  • Clinical workflow support and patient record access
  • Claims and NHIS processing support
  • Integration between hospital pharmacy and broader facility operations

4. Why HealthFlow

  • Role-based access controls
  • Cloud deployment with offline support
  • Built for pharmacy, clinic, and hospital use
  • Supports claims, tariffs, and reporting
  • Supports growth across branches and facilities
